Hazarot runs as one celld writer with persistent storage.

Local access:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward service/{{ .Release.Name }} 9876:{{ .Values.service.port }}

Open the URL in your private hazarot-admin.json to enable creating rooms.
Check /health for creatorConfigured:true. No inference is required for authored play.

Your PVC is retained on uninstall. Take an offline backup before image upgrades.
Do not scale replicas or share the data directory between running instances.
